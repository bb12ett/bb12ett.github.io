PaperPop: Toddler Coloring Book

Android App • Kid-Safe Coloring & Drawing
🛡️ 100% Kid-Safe 🚫 Zero Ads ✈️ Offline Ready 🔒 Parent Gated

PaperPop is a simple, tactile coloring book app for toddlers and young children. It allows parents to turn real paper drawings into digital coloring pages for their children using a parent-gated on-device scanner, with zero ads, zero tracking, and toddler kiosk controls.

📷 Parent-Gated Paper Scanner Allows parents to snap paper drawings and convert them to clean line art directly on-device.
🔒 Toddler Kiosk Lock Locks navigation gestures so toddlers can color without accidentally leaving the app.
🖊️ Stylus & Finger Support Supports pressure sensitivity for stylus pens alongside intuitive finger painting.
🛡️ Private & Offline No user accounts, no telemetry, no third-party ads, and no internet connection needed.
