PaperPop: Toddler Coloring Book

Android • Kid-Safe Creative Studio

✨ 100% Ad-Free 🛡️ Zero Data Collection ✈️ Full Offline Support 🔒 Parent-Gated Controls

PaperPop combines the charm of real-world coloring with interactive digital painting. Built specially for curious toddlers and peace-of-mind for parents.

📷 Magic Paper Scanner Snap a photo of any coloring book or doodle to turn it instantly into a clean digital outline on-device.
🔒 Toddler Kiosk Mode Prevents accidental exits, status bar pulls, and navigation swipes while little hands are painting.
🖊️ Stylus & Finger Support Full pressure sensitivity and palm rejection for stylus pens, plus easy, intuitive finger painting.
🛡️ Private & Offline No accounts, no third-party tracking, and no internet connection required to enjoy.
