PaperPop: Toddler Coloring Book

Android App • Kid-Safe Coloring & Drawing
🛡️ 100% Kid-Safe 🚫 Zero Ads ✈️ Offline Ready 🔒 Parent Gated

PaperPop is a simple, tactile coloring book app for toddlers and young children. It allows parents to turn real paper drawings into digital coloring pages for their children using a parent-gated on-device scanner, with zero ads, zero tracking, and toddler kiosk controls.

📷 Parent-Gated Paper Scanner Allows parents to snap paper drawings and convert them to clean line art directly on-device.
🔒 Toddler Kiosk Lock Locks navigation gestures so toddlers can color without accidentally leaving the app.
🖊️ Stylus & Finger Support Supports pressure sensitivity for stylus pens alongside intuitive finger painting.
🛡️ Private & Offline No user accounts, no telemetry, no third-party ads, and no internet connection needed.

HABit: Household Budget Planner

Home Assistant Add-on • Private Payday-Anchored Cashflow Manager
🏠 Home Assistant 🔒 100% Self-Hosted 📅 Payday-Anchored 🔑 Optional BYOK Open Banking 👥 Multi-User PINs

HABit is an independent, self-hosted household budget and cashflow manager designed specifically for Home Assistant. Anchored around real payday cycles rather than artificial calendar months, HABit tracks multi-account cashflows, schedules credit card autopay settlements, shifts bills around public holidays, and offers optional direct-to-provider Open Banking synchronisation with zero developer servers.

📅 Real Payday Schedules Dynamically splits monthly cycles into 4 or 5 structured weeks from payday to payday.
👥 Household & Privacy PINs Joint budget oversight with personal 4-digit PIN locks for individual checking and salary privacy.
💳 Cashflow & Autopay Intelligence Real-time tracking of Current, Credit, and Savings accounts with automated credit card payoff rules.
🧮 Interactive Calculator (Alt+C) Built-in floating calculator with a click-to-pick Value Picker to pull figures directly from your budget.
