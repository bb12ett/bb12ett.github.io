Privacy Policy

PaperPop: Toddler Coloring Book
Package: com.bb12ett.paperpop.coloring • Published by bb12ett (Brett)
Effective Date: September 2026
Our Commitment to Families & Young Children: PaperPop was built from the start with privacy as an absolute priority. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or analytics logs. The app functions 100% offline.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PaperPop does not collect or process any personal information. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user accounts
  • No GPS location or IP geolocation data
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • No user behavior telemetry, event tracking, or analytics
  • No voice recordings, audio clips, or biometric data

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

PaperPop complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Data Protection Act, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's strict Families Policy:

  • No personal data is ever requested or gathered from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK).
  • The application operates entirely without user accounts or cloud syncing.
  • All external outbound links (such as this Privacy Policy or support email) are protected behind an adult-only Parental Gate (an arithmetic problem) to prevent unaccompanied external navigation.

3. Device Permissions & Camera Usage

PaperPop requests only the bare minimum Android permissions necessary to support its drawing and coloring features:

  • Camera / Photos Access: Used exclusively to allow a parent or toddler to take a picture of a physical paper drawing or select an existing coloring page to convert it into a digital line outline.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: All image processing, edge detection algorithms, and outline thresholding occur entirely in the device's local memory using on-device computer vision. Camera frames and pictures are never transmitted to any external server, cloud platform, or third party.
  • Local Storage: Saved artworks and coloring pages are saved solely into the app's sandboxed local storage on your device.

4. Zero Advertising & Zero Third-Party SDKs

PaperPop contains zero third-party advertisements and zero tracking libraries:

  • No banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, rewarded videos, or promotional links.
  • No analytics SDKs, Meta/Facebook SDKs, Firebase Analytics, or cross-app tracking beacons.

5. In-App Purchases (PaperPop Pro)

PaperPop offers an optional, one-time digital purchase to unlock the full template library and unlimited paper scans:

  • All purchase processing is handled directly by Google Play In-App Billing.
  • The developer (bb12ett) never sees, accesses, receives, or stores your credit card details or billing address.
  • The purchase screen is gated behind the adult Parental Gate.

6. Data Security and Deletion

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted remotely, no personal data is retained on external servers. All saved pictures, color palettes, and cached templates remain strictly on your physical Android device. You can erase all application data at any time through Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.
