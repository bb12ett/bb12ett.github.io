Privacy Policy

PaperPop: Toddler Coloring Book (com.bb12ett.paperpop.coloring)
Published by bb12ett • Last updated: September 2026
Our Commitment to Families: PaperPop was designed from the ground up to protect children's safety and privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information or device identifiers. The app functions completely offline.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe young children deserve an entirely private, safe digital space. PaperPop does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact information
  • Location data (GPS, network location, or IP-based geolocation)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Behavioral analytics, telemetry, or user activity logs
  • Audio recordings or biometric information

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

PaperPop strictly complies with the United States Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European Union & UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy.

Because the app is directed toward children under the age of 13:

  • No personal data is knowingly requested, gathered, or retained.
  • No user accounts, logins, or profiles exist within the app.
  • Outbound links (such as this Privacy Policy or support email) are safeguarded behind a Parental Gate (an adult arithmetic challenge) so children cannot navigate outside the app unattended.

3. Device Permissions & Camera Usage

PaperPop requests only the essential permissions required to deliver core functionality:

  • Camera & Photos: Used solely to allow a parent or child to photograph a real-world coloring page, drawing, or picture to convert it into a digital coloring outline.
  • Local Processing Only: All image outline processing (edge detection and filtering) is performed 100% on-device in local memory. Photos and camera frames are never transmitted to external servers, cloud providers, or third parties.
  • Drawing Gallery Storage: Saved coloring artworks are stored locally on the device's internal storage and remain entirely under the user's control.

4. Advertising and Tracking SDKs

PaperPop contains zero third-party advertisements and zero tracking SDKs:

  • No banner ads, pop-ups, interstitial ads, or rewarded video ads.
  • No third-party tracking libraries, analytics trackers, or social media SDKs.

5. In-App Purchases & Billing

PaperPop offers an optional one-time digital purchase to unlock the full template library and unlimited scanner functionality ("PaperPop Pro"):

  • All financial transactions are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play In-App Billing.
  • The developer (bb12ett) never sees, accesses, or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.
  • Access to the purchase screen is protected behind an adult-only Parental Gate.

6. Data Security and Retention

Since PaperPop does not collect or transmit personal data to external servers, no personal data is retained or stored remotely. Any user drawings, cached templates, or app preferences are stored exclusively on your local device and can be erased at any time by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update our Privacy Policy, the revised policy will be published at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. We will never introduce data collection or advertising in child-directed versions of PaperPop.
