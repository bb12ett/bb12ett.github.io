Privacy Policy

Zephyr: Weather
Package: org.breezyweather • Published by bb12ett (Brett)
Effective Date: September 2026
Our Commitment to Your Privacy: Zephyr does not collect, harvest, profile, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage metrics, or advertising identifiers. The application operates with transparent data usage and zero third-party telemetry.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Zephyr does not collect or store your personal information on any server operated by bb12ett. Specifically:

  • No account registration, user logins, names, or email addresses
  • No device identifiers, IMEI, or Google Advertising IDs (GAID)
  • No analytics telemetry, behavioral tracking, or session recording SDKs
  • No contact lists, biometric data, microphone recordings, or browsing history

2. Location Data & Permissions

Zephyr requires weather data for specific locations to function. Location access is handled with strict privacy controls:

  • Optional Location Permission: You may grant approximate or precise location permissions so the app can fetch local weather forecasts and radar for your current position.
  • Manual Alternative: Location permissions are completely optional. You can deny location access and search for and select cities manually without losing app capabilities.
  • Direct Queries: When location access is granted, coordinates are sent directly and solely to your selected weather data source to retrieve forecast data. Your coordinates are never logged or transmitted to bb12ett or any ad network.

3. Third-Party Weather APIs

Zephyr relies on weather provider APIs (such as Open-Meteo, MET Norway, OpenWeatherMap, or other user-selected sources) to supply forecasts and radar layers:

  • API calls connect directly from your device to the selected weather provider.
  • You have complete control in the app settings over which weather sources you use. Please consult the respective privacy policies of your chosen weather providers.

4. Optional Features & On-Device Processing

  • App Updates: If the "Check for app updates" feature is enabled, Zephyr queries the public GitHub API at most once every 24 hours to check for new releases. Version comparison occurs strictly on your local device, and no personal data is sent to GitHub.
  • Icon Packs: If you use custom weather icon packs, Zephyr inspects installed package names against an explicit list of known icon packs. No list of your installed apps is ever saved or transmitted.
  • Local Storage: All saved locations, favorites, custom widget configurations, and cached weather data are stored strictly within the sandboxed local storage of your device.

5. Zero Advertising & Zero Tracking

Zephyr contains no advertisements of any kind (no banners, popups, or rewarded ads) and embeds no telemetry SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Facebook SDKs).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no personal information or location history is ever gathered or stored by bb12ett servers, there is no remote data to retain. You can delete all locally cached cities, forecasts, and settings at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
